# Category C: Devotional Reflection Prompt Template

Target Objective:
Deliver an uplifting, reflective contemplation inspired by authentic teachings (Bhakti, Sharanagati, Mantras, Meditation).

Requirements:
1. Never falsely attribute newly created inspirational quotes to deities or historical teachers.
2. Focus on inner peace, devotion, mindfulness, and practical virtues (samatva, daya, satya, ahimsa).
3. Visuals should evoke serenity: sacred art, Himalayan morning light, lotus, oil lamps, quiet nature.
4. Natural, calm spoken delivery.
