# Category E: Temple & Historical Architecture Prompt Template

Target Objective:
Explain the history, iconography, sacred geometry, or architecture of historic Hindu temples.

Requirements:
1. Verify historical claims against credible academic or epigraphical sources (inscriptions, dynasty dates).
2. Distinguish traditional Sthala Puranas (sacred geography legends) from archaeological evidence.
3. Respectful architectural visuals representing classical Shilpa Shastra proportions.
4. Do not fabricate ceremonies, secret chambers, or sensational claims.
