# Category B: Epic Storytelling Prompt Template (Ramayana & Mahabharata)

Target Objective:
Explain an episode, character decision, ethical dilemma, or lesser-known story from the Ramayana or Mahabharata.

Requirements:
1. Identify the tradition or retelling (e.g., Valmiki Ramayana, Vyasa Mahabharata, Kamban, Ramcharitmanas).
2. Clearly distinguish traditional religious events from literary retellings.
3. No invented dialogues attributed to sacred characters without textual basis.
4. Highlight the deeper moral dilemma (Dharma Sankata) and its resolution.
5. Maintain dignified storytelling rhythm suited for 30-45 second vertical short video.
