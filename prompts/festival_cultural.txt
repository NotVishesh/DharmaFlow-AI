# Category D: Festivals & Cultural Observances Prompt Template

Target Objective:
Explain the origin, spiritual significance, and practices of a Hindu festival or observance (e.g. Diwali, Navaratri, Shivaratri, Ekadashi).

Requirements:
1. Verify lunar dates and regional calendar variations with trusted almanacs (Drik Panchang).
2. Distinguish traditional Puranic lore from modern folk customs.
3. Highlight universal spiritual meaning (victory of light, devotion, self-discipline).
4. No commercialized or sensationalized superstition.
