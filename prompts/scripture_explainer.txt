# Category A: Bhagavad Gita & Scripture Explainer Prompt Template

Target Objective:
Explain a specific shloka or philosophical concept from authentic scripture (Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads) in simple Hindi/English with practical life application.

Requirements:
1. Exact citation (Chapter number and Verse number).
2. Sanskrit verse quotation (must be verifiable against classical editions).
3. Clear distinction between:
   - Original Sanskrit text
   - Exact translation
   - Philosophical interpretation
   - Practical modern takeaway
4. Opening hook must be informative and truthful (no clickbait, no fear of curses).
5. Respectful depiction in vertical 9:16 portrait composition.
6. Target duration: 30-45 seconds.
